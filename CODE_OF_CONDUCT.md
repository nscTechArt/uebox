# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / 贡献者行为准则

## Our Pledge / 我们的承诺

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

作为社区成员、贡献者和维护者，我们承诺让每一个人都能无骚扰地参与本项目，
无论其年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、
社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、种姓、肤色、宗教信仰或性取向。

我们承诺以有助于建立开放、友好、多元、包容、健康社区的方式行事与交流。

## Our Standards / 我们的准则

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

有助于建立积极环境的行为包括：

- 对他人抱有同理心与善意
- 尊重不同的意见、观点与经历
- 给出并优雅地接受建设性反馈
- 为自己的错误负责，并向受影响的人道歉
- 不只关注个人利益，而是关注整个社区的利益

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission
- **Belittling someone for using AI to contribute, or for the level of their coding experience**
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

不可接受的行为包括：

- 使用性化的语言或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或性挑逗
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论，以及人身或政治攻击
- 公开或私下的骚扰
- 未经明确许可，公开他人的私人信息（如住址、电子邮箱）
- **因为他人使用 AI 参与贡献、或因为其编程经验水平而贬低对方**
- 其他在专业场合中可合理认定为不当的行为

## Enforcement Responsibilities / 执行责任

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable
behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

社区维护者有责任解释并执行上述准则，并对任何被认为不当、威胁、冒犯或有害的行为
采取适当且公正的纠正措施。

## Scope / 适用范围

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is
officially representing the community in public spaces.

本准则适用于所有社区空间，也适用于个人在公开场合正式代表本社区时。

## Enforcement / 举报方式

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainers through GitHub's private
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
flow, or by opening a private
[Security Advisory](../../security/advisories/new) if the matter is sensitive.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

如需举报辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，可通过 GitHub 的
[举报滥用](https://docs.github.com/cn/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
流程联系维护者；涉及敏感内容时，也可通过私有
[Security Advisory](../../security/advisories/new) 提交。
所有举报都会被及时、公正地审查与处理。
