## Who can enter

- Age 18+, an active Epic Games account that is not a cabined account.
- Solo or up to five people; any number of AI agents. One submission per team.
- Unreal Engine **or** UEFN, not both.
- Restricted jurisdictions are listed at the top of the Official Rules (Iran, Iraq, North Korea,
  Somalia, Sudan, Syria, Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk, plus places where prizes cannot legally be
  paid). Mainland China is not on the list.
- Student prize: every team member must be enrolled at a higher-education institution.
- Prizes are paid to the Team Owner, who splits them.

## What to submit (Unreal Engine)

1. A **packaged Windows Development build**, playable on Windows 10/11 (macOS, Android or iOS
   also accepted). Unpackaged projects are rejected. The game must run without extra software.
2. A zip named `TeamName_GameName`, under 1 GB (larger: butler or Blendo Itch Uploader).
3. Uploaded to the team's own itch.io account, then submitted to the jam with:
   - team name, every member's name and the email of their Epic account, one marked as Team Owner;
   - game name;
   - gameplay video link, 30–60 seconds;
   - dev log link (optional; required for the Glass Box prize);
   - the list of sourced (non-original) assets.
4. **Everything in English**: in-game text, audio, the video and the entry information.
5. Gameplay must use the theme and go beyond Epic's starter templates.
6. No trademarks or logos except the Unreal Engine logo.

## Judging

Each qualified entry is scored 1–5 on:

| Criterion | What judges look for |
| --- | --- |
| Ambition and innovation | A new mechanic, a bold art style or a bigger scope than four days usually allow |
| Theme | A fresh, clever take on the theme |
| Fun factor | Fun and satisfying to play |
| Gameplay | Solid, original mechanics that feel good |
| Visuals | A strong, consistent look, photoreal or stylized |
| Level design and progression | Spaces that support the gameplay and guide the player |
| Audio | Sound and music that add to the experience |

## Prepared before the jam

Allowed before Nov 12: AI tools, agent configuration, MCP settings, prompt libraries, scripts.
Epic may ask for these materials or for version-control history. Not allowed: gameplay, levels
or art made before the start. Declare everything prepared earlier (template in
`submission.md`).

## MegaJam on Box Plan

Two steps, both inside UE Box only (no page on the web), both on the account UE Box is
connected to. Each team member does them with their own account.

1. **Register** — Settings → Models → Box Plan → Connect, then the "MegaJam 参赛登记" row →
   itch.io profile → 登记 (or `box_plan` `claim_megajam`). Adds a small trial credit for 7 days.
   One registration per account and per itch.io profile; the profile must exist. Closes at the
   submission deadline or when all spots are taken.
2. **Submit** — after the game page is public on itch.io and the jam form is submitted: the same
   row → itch.io game page → 交卷 (or `box_plan` `submit_megajam_entry`). Every team member
   submits the same game page from their own UE Box. Resubmitting replaces the earlier link.

After the jam the entries are checked against the jam's submission list. For each entry, what
the account spent through UE Box during the jam, minus the trial credit, is added back to its
Box Plan balance (capped per person). Only usage through UE Box's own Box Plan connection
counts; keys created on the website do not. To get the most from it, the player can buy a Box
Plan subscription or top up for the jam.

- Error messages: wrong link format (profile `https://name.itch.io`, game page
  `https://name.itch.io/game`), profile or game page not found on itch.io (a Draft page is not
  found — make it public first), profile registered by another account, already registered,
  all taken, closed.
- When the credit runs out, top up or switch models in Settings → Models; nothing in the
  project is lost.
